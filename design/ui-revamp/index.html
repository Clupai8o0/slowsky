Slowsky

Four ways to make Slowsky easier.

The first three came out of a debate between Codex, Gemini, Grok and Claude; each was built by a different model. The fourth, Tool, works like an app: one screen, a toolbar, and a details panel that opens only when you ask.

1 · built by Gemini

Theatre

One centred stage. The sky blinks as soon as the page opens. Details slide up as sheets, so nothing sits beside the sky.

Open → 2 · built by Codex

Guided tour

One column of short steps. Each step adds one control just when you need it. "Explore freely" shows every control at once.

Open → 3 · built by Grok

Contact sheet

Every visit side by side at the same scale, so you see the change before pressing Play. Pick any two dates to blink.

Open → 4 · shell by Claude, engine by Codex, app by Gemini

Tool

A full-screen workbench instead of a page. Upload your own FITS, PNG or JPG and it is read, lined up and compared in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
