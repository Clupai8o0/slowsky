# Design debate: result (15 Nov, Codex, Gemini Argon, Grok 4.7, Claude Opus)

**Outcome: build three single-column directions, none with side panels: Theatre, Guided tour, Contact sheet.**

| Round 1 proposal | By | Idea in one line |
|---|---|---|
| Sky First Workspace | Codex | Big viewer + one explanation panel; press Play to start |
| Guided Exhibit Workbench | Gemini | Story column left, sky + one time-and-colour dock right |
| Open on the blink | Grok | Only a blinking sky and one sentence; everything else in drawers |
| Plate and inspector | Opus | Blinking sky with one sentence; tabs on the right for the rest |

| Round 2 vote (best first) | Codex | Gemini | Grok | Opus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | Codex | Gemini | Opus | Opus |
| 2nd | Gemini | Opus | Gemini | Gemini |
| 3rd | Opus | Codex | Grok | Codex |
| 4th | Grok | Grok | Codex | Grok |

Sharpest attacks:
- Codex's page starts still: the visitor must find Play before anything moves (Gemini, Grok, Opus).
- Gemini's instruction sits a column away from the sky, so you lose which dot to watch (Opus).
- Grok hides colour in a drawer, so "Dim at some colours" has no visible control (everyone).
- Opus buries "What this can't tell you" in a tab (Codex, Gemini).

All four agreed:
- The sky starts blinking on arrival (paused if the device asks for reduced motion).
- Blinking must hard-cut, not fade: a fade shows the star twice and fakes a brightness dip.
- Loads keep the old frame until the new one is ready, with a thin progress line; nothing jumps.
- The caveat for a target sits right under the sky, not in a hidden panel.

Your notes folded in: no side panels, loading animation on every control that loads a frame, smoother
transitions, a livelier centre, and many interactive elements (sky map, loupe, hold-to-compare, scrubber).

| Direction | Built by | Core idea |
|---|---|---|
| Theatre | Gemini Carbon | One centred stage; details slide up as sheets |
| Guided tour | Codex | One column of short steps, each adding one control |
| Contact sheet | Grok 4.7 | All visits side by side; blink any two |
