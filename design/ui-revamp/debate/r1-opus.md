## Name (2-4 words)
Plate and inspector

## Core idea (one sentence)
The sky is one dark "plate" on a light page, already blinking, with one plain sentence under it; everything else moves into one right-hand inspector.

## First 10 seconds (what the visitor sees and does, step by step)
1. Barnard's Star loads already blinking (paused under reduced motion).
2. Under the plate: "Watch the gold ring: the star steps to it on each date."
3. Motion seen in two seconds; they click "RW Aurigae" in the target tabs.
4. Plate and sentence swap; the colour slider is next in reach.

## Desktop layout (regions, what lives where)
- Top bar: logo, search "Any star, or RA, Dec" (⌘K), Copy link, theme.
- Target tabs: five names, active one underlined.
- Stage (2/3): title, sentence, plate, dated timeline with thumbnails, transport, colour row.
- Inspector (1/3) tabs: **Guide** (diagrams, coordinates, "What this can't tell you"), **Colour** (µm, detector), **Details** (frame table, FITS, candidate changes, Go live).

## Phone layout
Full-bleed plate, sentence, sticky bottom transport. Inspector becomes three accordions, Guide open. At 320 px, mode labels become icons.

## What you hide or cut (and where it goes instead)
Cut: section numerals, blue glow. Filmstrip becomes the timeline. Gaia DR3, detector, release, file, FITS go to Details. Field size and checkboxes go to a Layers popover. Go live sits beside the candidate changes it unlocks.

## Controls (how blink / stack / subtract, frame stepping, colour and the overlays work; no pills)
- Square segmented control, 4 px corners: Blink, Stack, Subtract; hint below.
- Prev, Play, Next beside the timeline; Space, arrows, B/S/D keys.
- Colour: flat rectangular track, square handle; below 0.75 µm greyed as "your eyes".
- Layers: square button on the plate corner; square checkboxes, field-size select.

## Light and dark (how the dark sky image sits in a light UI)
The plate stays near-black in both modes inside a 1 px frame, so ring contrast never changes. Light: warm off-white page. Dark: #0B0C10 page, lighter frame. Gold marks rings only.

## Riskiest assumption
That one sentence explains enough; if nobody opens Guide, they miss "What this can't tell you".
