I'll read the brief and the two screenshots first, then propose one direction.## Name
Open on the blink

## Core idea
A light page whose only job is a sky already blinking the widest pair of frames, with one sentence under it; everything else is a drawer.

## First 10 seconds
1. Warm-gray page. Top bar: stripe logo, Slowsky, and "Barnard's Star" as underlined text.
2. A dark square of real pixels hard-cuts between Aug 2025 and Aug 2026. Gold rings are on. Under the frame: "It walks north. The ring is where it should be on each date."
3. The visitor only watches. Space pauses.
4. If the system asks for reduced motion, the latest frame holds and a square Play sits on the frame's lower edge.

## Desktop layout
One top bar: logo and name on the left; target, "0.85 µm", Copy link, and a square theme mark on the right. No 01 / 02 / 03.
The stage is centered. The image caps near 640 px inside a near-black tray with 12 px of padding.
Under it, one row: Blink / Stack / Subtract, then the date stepper, then three dated thumbnails.
A narrow right rail opens 320 px drawers for Look for, Colour, Overlays, and This frame. They cover the margin. They do not squeeze the sky. The target list and its search open from the name, as a left drawer.

## Phone layout
The stage is nearly edge to edge, sentence directly under it. Blink, Stack, Subtract and prev / pause / next pin to the bottom. Thumbnails scroll sideways. Target, colour, overlays, the frame table, and the written guide share one bottom sheet with underlined text tabs, stopping near 45% so the sky stays visible.

## What you hide or cut
Cut the numbered columns and the always-visible essays. Diagrams, "what to look for", and "what this can't tell you" go in Look for. Detector, release, FITS, file, scale, candidate changes, and Go live go in This frame. The first load uses the saved copy and does not say so. Flagged pixels, false colour, older picture, and field size go in Overlays, off except the expected-position ring and the brightest-star rings. Search exists only inside the target drawer.

## Controls
Blink, Stack, and Subtract are three squares in one 1 px frame. The active square is a solid fill. Corners are 2 px. Blink hard-cuts every 800 ms between the first and last visit only. Stack freezes the stepper. Subtract changes the sentence to "Bright where this date differs from the first." ‹ date › and the arrow keys step frames. A thumbnail jumps. Colour is a square-thumbed track in its drawer, read as "0.85 µm · detector 1 of 6", with ticks from 0.4 to 5. Overlays are a list of square checkboxes, each with one line of why.

## Light and dark
The canvas is #f4f4f2 with near-black Geist. Pixels stay black in a #0c0e12 tray and a 1 px hairline. No glow. Dark mode keeps the layout on #0e0f12. The tray drops its outer line and separates by space. The wavelength track does not invert. Theme is a square icon: follow the system once, then remember. No switch.

## Riskiest assumption
A few pixels of motion, blinked with no coaching on screen, reads as a star walking. If the jump looks like a glitch, the hidden guide was the thing a first visit needed.
